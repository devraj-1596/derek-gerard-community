Who is Derek?

Derek Gerard is a Canadian YouTuber who built his audience on 24-hour challenges and the Impossible Food Challenge series, often alongside best friend Jaspal Sidhu.

His channel is full of rules he probably regrets: eating whatever a dog, a stranger or a thumbnail decides, saying yes to everything, buying random stuff.

In July 2023 he launched That Library Show: whispered, ASMR-style interviews filmed at the Vancouver Public Library with creators and other guests.

His fans go by the D-Squad, and this site is for them.
